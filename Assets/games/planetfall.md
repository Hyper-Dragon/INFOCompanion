### Find out if you're Stellar Patrol material — today

The booklet's four-page centre section is a **recruitment questionnaire**, and it is the part of the
package a player is most likely to have filled in. Its preamble:

> This incredibly comprehensive questionnaire was prepared totally in accordance with the rules and
> regulations of the Eighth Division Codes of the Third Galactic Union.
>
> To help your recruitment officer determine the best positions for you when you join the Stellar Patrol,
> fill out the entire questionnaire honestly and without help from family members or friends.
>
> Note: Although most of this data is on Permafile at Third Galactic Headquarters and can be verified
> instantly, this is our only method for determining how closely you adhere to the standard code of honor.

**PSYCHOLOGICAL PROFILE** opens with the questionnaire's best-known question:

> 1. Which of the following would you be willing to do for your Union?
> ☐ die
> ☐ die slowly
> ☐ die slowly and painfully
> ☐ read an Infocom instruction manual
> ☐ none of the above

and continues into *"I am most attracted to: beings who are superior to myself in rank / beings of the
opposite sex / beings of the same sex / beings of no sex / myself"*, mental disorders that might
disqualify you from laboratory experiments (*"it doesn't matter; I'll do whatever I'm told"*, *"no"*,
*"definitely not"*), and favourite recreations: *"mopping up after slimy beings who are superior to
myself in rank"*, *"dueling with laser bazookas at two paces"*.

The questionnaire's other pages ask for colour of eyes, colour of hair and height — with a tick-box for
*"Below 1.5 meters but willing to undergo Artificial Elongation Therapy to meet Stellar Patrol
requirements"* — respiratory function (*nose / mouth / both / neither / none of the above*), whether you
smoke (*often / sometimes / never / never looked*), and overall physical health, whose last option is
*"Notify my next of kin immediately."* They also ask what you enjoy working with — *people? animals?
plants? aliens? finger paints?* — whether you can communicate *in Galalingua / in monosyllabic grunts /
via Astronmet's Universal Sign Language / not at all*, and, in ten words or less, *"describe the very
reason for your existence"*.

The three self-administered factors are the best joke in the booklet, and a player with a pencil could
actually fail them:

> 1. Patience factor: Stand in a corner of the room facing the wall for as long as you can. Don't
> continue reading until you stop. Now, write here how long you stood: ______ (in days).
>
> 2. Hydrophobia factor: Chain yourself to a rock underwater for as long as you can. Don't continue
> reading until you stop. Now, write here how long you held your breath: ______ (in days).
>
> 3. Monotony factor: Repeat number 17 above as many times as you can. Don't continue reading until you
> stop. Now, write here whether you were really gullible enough to repeat number 17: ______.

**LOGICAL REASONING ABILITIES** gives three analogies with deliberately awful answer lists — *"FOOT is to
SHOE as FINGER is to: a. Nose / b. Eye / c. Ear / d. Mouse / e. Donut / f. Honesty"*; *"RAIN is to SNOW as
GROTCH is to: […] f. 3.14159"*; *"HULL is to SPACESHIP as SKIN is to: […] f. Muffin"* — followed by
fill-in-the-blank history questions (*"Who invented the light deceleration process known as slow
glass?"*, *"Name the year in which Arnold Guunuf invented slow glass"*), a question whose answer is *"What
is the answer to this question?"*, and two real puzzle problems: a circular-table seating problem for
**three couples (the Phariixes, the Boarbs and the Keqrees)** — a cosmobiologist, a gravity engineer, a
sleep technician, an ambassador, a fusion supervisor and an editor, originally from *Gallium, Leglama,
Granjil-6, Storvbay, Ansill, and Jaaggo* — and a problem about four robotic satellites (**YA3, JP7, SEM6,
MD8**) meant to find, transport, convert and distribute drifting garbage.

The instruction at the end of the questionnaire:

> Submit this completed questionnaire to a Stellar Patrol recruiter. If you qualify for the Patrol, you
> will be notified within two chrons.

## The setting and the organisations, as the package prints them

The facts a player had before sitting down. The galaxy is governed by the **Third Galactic Union**,
formed in **11,203 GY** by the **Great Treaty** between the **Empires of Tremain and Gallium** and centred
on **Tremain**; it replaced a **Second Galactic Union** that fell in **1716 GY**, after which came a
ten-thousand-year dark age. Its "pseudo-military wing" is the **Stellar Patrol**, with uniforms designed
on **Vandermeer**, the motto **"Boldly Go Where Angels Fear to Tread"**, and a **Special Assignment Task
Force**. Its ranks run from **Cadet 4th Class** and **Ensign Seventh Class** up to **Ensign First Class**;
its ships carry the prefix **S.P.S.**, and the player's is the **S.P.S. Feinstein**. Its clock divides the
day into ten thousand **millichrons**.

The player's printed circumstances: a native of **Gallium**, five generations of Patrol service behind
them, transferred aboard the **Feinstein** under **Ensign First Class Blather** and still **Ensign Seventh
Class**, sent out of hyperspace early to investigate a planetary system that archaeologists on **Varshon**
believe was part of the Second Union — and a mission that begins, per the box, with the ship destroyed,
the ensign crash-landing, and **Floyd** the multi-purpose robot as company.

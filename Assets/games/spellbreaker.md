## What is not in the scans

So that the next reader does not go hunting for pages that were never photographed: there is **no
manual apart from pages 11 and the blue page**, **no printed map**, **no disk or disk label**, **no
Enchanter's Guild pin** except as an object in the box-back photograph, **no box front or back spine
strip with a platform line**, and **no catalogue cover, page 1 or page 10 onward** — the catalogue
scan set jumps from the cover photograph on the box straight to the spread numbered 2–3, and stops
after the spread numbered 8–9. The scan files themselves run `frobozz2` through `frobozz8` with
`frobozz1` and `frobozz6` absent.

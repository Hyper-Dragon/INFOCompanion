* **Spells with their printed gloss** — FILFRE "display gratuitous fireworks"; GONDAR "extinguish
  fire"; ZOOKA "turn eggs into overripe cabbage"; STEGAW "turn eggs into ripe guano"; LOBAL
  "sharpen hearing"; CONBAK "build strong bodies 12 different ways"; FAIFT "change appearance to
  look younger"; IMALI "worsen eyesight"; OTSUNG "erase spell written in book with GNUSTO";
  GASPAR, a *guano-safe* resurrection spell, with the footnote that OZMOR "is not considered to be
  a true resurrection spell"; KULCAD, the scroll a classified ad wants badly enough to pay top
  dollar for.
* **Spells named without a gloss** — FOBLUB, cast on an audience to glue them to their seats;
  GNUSTO, which writes other spells into a book so they can be cast again but cannot copy extremely
  powerful spells; CLEESH, what Enchanters do "first"; SIRANO, a potion that supplies the poetry
  you cannot; BAYALA and MUSDEX, called body-deforming spells; REZROV; BLORB; QUELBO.
* **On Nate's price list** — scrolls FROTZ, REZROV, BLORB, NITFOL, QUELBO, GRICPO, YOMIN, ONKIK,
  YIMFIL and VEZZA; potions BERZIO, IGNATZ, VILSTU, ONBIT, KWIN, WALDOE, KNALB and BLORT; magic
  amulets; wands marked BII, UT and SS; scroll and wand racks; mortar and pestle; tongue of owl; jar
  of newt eyes; jar of ground morgia; bat wings; toad warts; dragon scales.
* **CHEVAUX** is not a spell but a brand of spell book, Mithican leather bound, sold by the
  bookbinder Priggin in Mizia; the **infotater** is the rotating data wheel the Field Guide stands
  in for, the Frobozz Magic Grommet Company having gone bankrupt.
* **People** — Belboz the Necromancer (Guildmaster of the Accardi Chapter, kingdomwide Secretary of
  the Guild, destroyer of the giant Amatradonis); Krill, the Evil Warlock; the Circle and the Guild
  of Enchanters; Barbel of Gurth; Orkan of Thriff; Berzio, inventor of the GNUSTO spell; Wilbar
  Memboob; Elvis Flathead; Nate.
* **Firms** — United Thaumaturgy; Smoothscroll Draughtsmen; Spellshack; International Business
  Magic; the Spell Science Lab at G.U.E. Tech; the Frobozz Magic Grommet Company; Borphee Infotaters,
  Inc.; Nymph-O-Mania.
* **Places** — Antharia, Gurth, Mithicus, Thriff, Borphee, Greater Borphee, Borphee Harbor, Accardi,
  Accardi-by-the-Sea, Aragain Falls, the Frigid River Valley, the Gray Mountains, the Flathead Ocean,
  the Land of Frobozz, Mizia, the Zilton Hotel, the Rusty Knife in West Anthar, the Great Underground
  Empire.
* **Money and creatures** — the **zorkmid** (*zm*); the twelve creatures of the Field Guide, plus
  the paskalds (which a kobold can be mistaken for) and the Hi-Res Orcs.

## How these papers sit beside the game

The Field Guide is what the player kept at hand: twelve creatures, each with picture, behaviour and
a closing list of colours. *Popular Enchanting* is the world's own newspaper, where the Guild's
quarrels, the price of a scroll and the effect of a spell are ordinary commerce. The booklet's last
page is the package's own account of the story: a mentor gone, a cryptic diary left behind, a search
through mazes, mines and the mists of time.

*What came in the Zork III box, transcribed from the package itself.*

The box lists its contents in a photograph on the back: *"Preserved for posterity inside this and
every ZORK III package are your ZORK III disk, FrobozzCo Annual Report, an official stock
certificate and a highly important-sounding letter from the Chairman of the Board."* Three of the
four are in the scans — the **Annual Report**, the **stock certificate** and the **letter**. The
instruction manual was not among them.

One note on the edition: the disk photographed and sold with this copy is the **Commodore Plus/4**
release — *"Recreational Software by Infocom T266003"* — and the box is marked **INTERACTIVE
FICTION / FANTASY / ADVANCED LEVEL**.

## The box, in its own words

**The front** is on plain black. Across the top, in white: **"It all comes down to this."** Below
it, the word **ZORK** built out of white stone blocks, and where the O should be, a **closed wooden
door with iron hinges and a latching ring**, standing open a crack with pale light on the other
side. The blocks sit on a stone arch, and beyond the arch the wall breaks away into rubble — the
empire at the end of itself.

**The back** gives the meeting, and it is the game's premise in one speech:

> An old, oddly youthful man turns toward you slowly. His long, silver hair dances about him as a
> fresh breeze blows. *"You have reached the final test, my friend! You are proved clever and
> powerful, but this is not yet enough! Seek me when you feel yourself worthy!"*
>
> **ZORK III: THE DUNGEON MASTER** draws you into the deepest and most mysterious reaches of the
> Great Underground Empire. **Nothing is as it seems.** And the one responsible for the shadow and
> darkness — the Dungeon Master — embodies the greatest mystery of all.
>
> In this test of wisdom and courage, you will face countless dangers. But what awaits you at the
> culmination of your odyssey is well worth risking all.

The rest of the back is Infocom's standard matter: the "Get inside a story" pitch, the three-level
difficulty key (**Junior**, **Standard**, **Advanced** — Zork III is the last), the publisher's
block (Infocom, 55 Wheeler St., Cambridge, MA 02138; © 1984 Infocom; ZORK is a registered trademark
of Infocom), and the ISBN 0-88731-029-X.

## A word about the paperwork

Everything in this box is a **joke with a straight face**, and it is a joke with the same target the
game has: the collapse of the Great Underground Empire. FrobozzCo International is a conglomerate
that owns everything (its annual report has a page of subsidiaries running to hundreds of names,
from the Frobozz Magic Anchor Company to the Frobozz Magic Zwieback Company), it is rich, and every
document in the box is being mailed to you as a **shareholder** on the strength of the stock
certificate. The plaque on the back of the report warns you about the Dungeon Master; the letter
inside thanks you for your continued support as an investor. Both are true. That is the tone of it.

## The FrobozzCo International Annual Report, 778 GUE

A bound report whose cover is a photograph of **wood-grained panel with iron hinges and clasps** and
a carved **F** monogram: *FrobozzCo International Annual Report, 778 GUE*.

**The chairman's page** carries a small illustration of the **Festeron Town Library** — a red-tiled
building with a card in the window, stamped with dates of a lifetime's borrowing (05/16/781,
01/32/811, 09/11/846, 02/07/902, 02/21/948) and the label *"Please take care of me — library books
belong to everyone"* — and it is the only thing in the report that is not the company talking about
itself. The text around it:

> 778 was a year of … subsidiaries. Gross … 22%. The employment … workforce of the Gre… tire …
> only by the …
>
> Following the comple[tion of] the **Royal Museum**, the **Frobozz Magic Cave Company**, our
> largest subsidiary, moved to increase its staff even more as the well-publicized **dam and
> volcano projects** moved into full gear. The Cave Company will also be creating the new
> **400-story FrobozzCo world headquarters in Flatheadia**, scheduled to open sometime in 781.
>
> **More than 18,000 additional subsidiaries were formed or taken over** during the last year,
> further increasing the scope of our industrial empire. **FrobozzCo now produces everything from
> aardvarks to zwieback.**
>
> I trust that you, as a stockholder, will take the time to read through this report and learn about
> at least a few of the many exciting things that are going on here at FrobozzCo. It's certainly
> been a tremendous year, but we're looking forward to an even **tremendouser** year in 779!
>
> **John D. Flathead**, President and Chairman of the Board, FrobozzCo International

**The subsidiary pages** are full-page advertisements, each with a caption tag in the corner, and
they are the best evidence of what this universe is made of:

* **FROBOZZ MAGIC CAVE COMPANY.** Illustrated with a shelf of trophies — a gold record or plaque,
  a golden cup, a pink wizard's cap with stars on it, a golden fish, a golden scroll — captioned
  *"The Frobozz Magic Cave Company has won an unqualified amount of honors, including the
  much-coveted **Dirtiest Shovel in the Great Underground Empire** award."* The text: the company
  was formed over a century ago to implement **King Dunccanrax's massive tunneling project**, and
  is now *"an umbrella for a wide range of underground construction projects"*. Two of the most
  ambitious are on the drawing board: **a dam on the Frigid River** — *"tentatively called **Flood
  Control Dam Number Three**"*, scheduled for completion in **783 G.U.E.** at a price tag of **37
  million zorkmids** — and a project to *"quench and then hollow out a mighty volcano"*, conducted
  under very tight security, with **Lord Dimwit Flathead** personally reviewing the plans at each
  stage. The company also created **46,000 linear blots of tunnel**, nearly **200 blots of Great
  Underground Highway extension**, and **8,000 cubic blots of additional cavern space**.
* **FROBOZZ MAGIC TWEEZERS COMPANY.** A giant pair of tweezers pinching a bee. Founded after a
  652,000-zorkmid market research survey found a widespread need for a high-quality technologically
  advanced tweezer. The **Model A-1** is rolling off the assembly lines, *"will retail for Zm 0.29"*;
  within six months a top-of-the-line **Model X-1** will be introduced at **Zm 0.89**; six more
  models are on the drawing board. The board is confident the division *"will pluck a lot of
  business for the parent conglomerate."*
* **FROBOZZ MAGIC SPELL COMPANY.** Illustrated with a *MAGIC SPELL CO.* book, jars, a retort and
  bubbling powder, captioned *"Some really amazing things materialize at the Frobozz Magic Spell
  Company."* The **well-heeled Enchanters Guild** remains the primary customer, but the invention of
  **self-casting spell scrolls** has created a huge new market. Sales rose 11% during 778, marking
  the twentieth year of steady growth, and several new divisions were formed to serve the demand —
  the Magic Scroll Rack Company, the Magic Spell Book Company and the Magic Scroll Mailing Tube
  Company. **Four new spells** were added to the product line this year, *"a Magic Spell Company
  record and a tribute to the wizards in FrobozzCo's famous Magiclab"*: **DRILLBO** (strips a floor
  of yellowed wax), **BORCH** (puts insects to sleep), **GIZGUM** (predicts visits by relatives) and
  **QUELBO** (transmutes coconuts into gold).
* **FROBOZZ MAGIC GRUE REPELLENT COMPANY.** A shelf of cans, tubes and sprays — **ANTI-GRUE KIT
  ("NEW IMPROVED")**, *GUESS?*, *GRUE CREAM*, *OIL REPELLENT GRUE*, *GRUE REPELLENT (NEW! LASTS
  UP TO 18 HOURS)*, *MEAT GRUE REPELLENT* — captioned *"Encouraged by the runaway success of their
  old socks/burning-rubber-scented repellent, company scientists increased the product line with
  seven new odors."* Sales nearly tripled last year thanks to *"drastic extensive improvements"*, and
  the company points to *"a 31% drop in grue-related deaths during the last year, and a consequent
  31% drop in the grue population"*. The new additive, **G-17**, is longer-lasting, requires fewer
  sprayings and can be made from ordinary sand. The line now includes, besides old socks/burning
  rubber: **rotting eggs, dead fish, swamp gas, three-week-old meatloaf, gym locker, wet dog and
  mint**. A **Free-Noseplugs-With-Every-Can** campaign with the Magic Noseplugs Company ran a month
  and increased sales by 92%, advertisements featuring **grue-mangled corpses** ran before and during
  the peak travel season, and a joint packaging effort with the Magic Lantern Company produced the
  Frobozz Anti-Grue Kit.
* **FROBOZZCO FINANCIAL REPORT.** Six line charts — **Sales, Profit, Number of Subsidiaries, Number
  of Employees, Name Recognition, Granola Subsidiaries** — each running smoothly up and to the right
  across the year axis, and then two tables, *"Zorkmid Amounts in Millions"*:

  The **Income Statement** for year end 778 and 777 lists revenue from *"Sales of goods and services"*
  (Zm 5,431,922 and 5,108,113), *"Sale of property"*, and *"Other revenues"*; expenses of *"Cost of
  goods and services"*, *"Selling, administration, **bribes**"*, *"Depreciation"*, *"**Contributions
  to Royal Charities**"* and *"Printing of Annual Report"*; and a net income of **Zm 1,277,419**
  against 571,873. Earnings per uncommon share: **Zm 2.69**. The **Balance Sheet** carries
  *"Loans to Royal Family"* (Zm 1,125,000) and an *"**Executive Party Fund**"* (107,574) among the
  assets, and *"Accrued payroll deductions"*, *"Deferred bribes"* and *"Other liabilities"* among
  the liabilities. The **Retained Earnings Statement** rounds it off.
* And the last page prints a painting of the **Original Headquarters Building, Constructed 667** — a
  timbered hall with towers, domes, a flag bearing the F, and a village around it in a green valley
  under snowy mountains — above the grey **F** monogram and the company's motto: *"You name it, we
  do it."*

**The back of the report** is where the joke stops being about money. It is a brass plaque screwed

> **If you have any doubts about your courage and wisdom, turn back now!**
>
> You have but a few steps to go before you enter the deepest and most mystical abyss of the Great

> He shall test your cleverness and bravery as he invents challenges the likes of which you've never
> known. His very presence defies explanation and is a great danger to you. If you prove yourself

> The Table of Contents for the manual is on page 11. Take a look at it to determine what you should

## The stock certificate

An engraved certificate in the style of a real one, with an ornate amber border of acanthus and
cornucopia work, the seal of **The Bank of Zork**, and a small engraving of a boy in a tunic
carrying a banner over rocky ground. Its heading:

> Incorporated under the laws of the **Great Underground Empire**. **UNCOMMON STOCK.** **ONE ZORKMID
> PER SHARE.** This certificate is transferable in the Empire or in any subcavern of said Empire.

Numbered **2-349-792-Z03**, it certifies that *[name]* **is the owner of 100 shares of fully paid
and non-assessable uncommon stock of the par value of one zorkmid per share**, countersigned by the
Bank of Zork and signed **John D. Flathead IX**, President and Chairman of the Board. The issuer, in
other words, is the same company whose annual report is in the box, and the holder — by the time
you have read this far — is you.

## The letter from the Chairman of the Board

One page, on the letterhead of **FrobozzCo International, 1533 Dimwit Boulevard, Borphpee, Greater
Borphpee province**, with the motto *"You name it, we do it!"* beneath the address and the F
monogram above it. Down the left-hand side runs a column of type so small it is a wall: the
**FrobozzCo International Subsidiaries** list, hundreds of names long, every one of them a Frobozz
Magic *something* Company. The letter itself is dated **9/33/947**:

> Dear Shareholder,
>
> It's been another banner year for FrobozzCo, as I'm sure you're aware. Everyone here on the
> FrobozzCo Management Team joins me in thanking you personally for your continued support as an
> investor.
>
> Thanks to our steady growth and record revenues in 947, the Board of Directors is pleased to
> announce a **two-for-one stock split**. You'll find your new shares enclosed.
>
> Don't forget about the shareholders' meeting at the Borphpee Civic Center on **2/1/948**. I hope
> you'll be on hand to approve the nomination of **John D. Flathead X** as the next Chairman of the
> Board. If you can't attend, please remember to return your proxy.
>
> Finally, as tremendous as fiscal 947 has been for us, your company is hoping that (as my
> great-great-great-great-great-grandfather was always fond of saying) this year will be even
> **tremendouser**!
>
> Sincerely,
>
> *John D. Flathead*
> John D. Flathead IX
> Chairman of the Board
> FrobozzCo International

Note the date, and the fact that the letter is signed by the **ninth** John D. Flathead while the
annual report — a hundred and seventy years earlier, in 778 — was signed by the first. The company
has been mailing shareholders the same good news ever since.

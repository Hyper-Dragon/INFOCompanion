## The journal: "Expedition Log — Ellingsworth Pyramid Search"

### The book itself

A marbled orange-and-brown composition notebook in black tape, with a white label on the front and on
the label, in orange handwriting: **Expedition Log / Ellingsworth / Pyramid Search**. A corner sticker

The journal opens on **July 8**, gives no year, and never gives its writer a name: he is the assistant

And below that, a two-column list headed **HIEROGLYPHIC SYMBOL · ENGLISH TRANSLATION**, giving ten

## The envelope

A single air-mail envelope on the same hotel paper, with the same **HÔTEL AMÉRICAIN** crest and
address line, and one Egyptian stamp (a building, **EGYPT** in Latin letters with Arabic beside it).
Along the left edge, **Air Mail** is written by hand between two hand-drawn arrows. The address is in
the letter's own hand:

> Rose Ellingsworth
> 55 Wheeler Street
> Cambridge, MA 02138
> USA

It is empty and unposted: the letter it belongs to is the one that ends in an unsigned scrawl.

## How these papers are meant to be used

The **map** and the **rubbing with its dictionary** are the equipment, meant to be read at the
keyboard; the **journal** and the **letters** are what is known *about* the man holding them, and the
box gives that as character rather than as clue. The boarding pass, the invoice and the IOU's are
where the money went; the clippings are what happened to the last man who tried this; the magazine and
the box back are the game's own advertising, and the narrator quotes one of them as evidence that
adventures are supposed to work out.

## What the player has in their hands

The guide and the brochures are tourist literature for the same Empire the story is set in, written
straight, with the joke being that FrobozzCo tours and prices in zorkmids are sold with the flat
enthusiasm of real holiday copy. Three things are worth noticing at the keyboard.

* **The guide covers provinces, not rooms.** Antharia, Greater Borphee, the Frigid River Valley, Gurth
  and Mithicus and the Gray Mountains each get government, transport, weather, lodging, dining,
  sights, seasonal events and a fact box. Nothing in it is a map of the game.
* **The two publications contradict each other in print.** The guide calls the Ski Pole a fleabag and
  recommends the Come-On Inn instead; the Grayslopes brochure recommends the Ski Pole Inn by name and
  rates it GGG. Bozbarland spells the village at its gates **Bozbarb** on the front and **Bozbarbo**
  on the back.
* **The guide's back cover is the only packaging that points at the manual** — *"The Table of Contents
  for the manuals is on page 11"* — and the only place in the guide that names **the Wizard of
  Frobozz**.
